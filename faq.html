Towbar Questions Answered

Towbar Installation FAQs

Clear answers to common questions about towbar selection, installation, pricing and mobile fitting in Melbourne.

How long does it take to fit a towbar?

Most vehicles take roughly two to three hours. Certain vehicles or more complex installations can require additional time.

Do you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. Advanced Towbars offers free on-site fitting across Melbourne, allowing the installation to be completed at your home or workplace.

How much does towbar installation cost?

The price varies according to the vehicle make and model, the towbar type and any additional equipment required. The team provides a personalised quote based on those factors.

Can you help me choose the right towbar?

Yes. Provide your vehicle details and what you intend to tow, and the team can recommend an appropriate solution.

Do you install towing electrics and brake controllers?

Advanced Towbars supplies towing equipment including electric brake controllers and related towing accessories.

Do you fit towbars to trucks?

Yes. Advanced Towbars offers light and heavy-duty truck towbar solutions and truck fitting services.

What other accessories do you supply?

The range includes tow hitches, sway control kits, roof and bike racks, reverse cameras, nudge and bull bars and selected 4WD accessories.

Which areas do you service?

The service area covers metropolitan Melbourne, including eastern, western, northern, inner-city and south-eastern areas, plus selected Victorian country towns.
