Fixed and removable solutions

The existing Advanced Towbars site describes fixed and removable-ball towbars. A removable solution can be useful where you prefer a cleaner rear profile when the towbar is not in use, while other applications favour a more permanent setup.

Light-duty and heavier towing applications

Vehicle and towbar ratings must be respected. Avoid choosing a towbar simply by appearance or a generic capacity figure: the vehicle, towbar and towing setup all need to be compatible.

Towbars are vehicle-specific

A towbar designed for one vehicle should not be assumed to fit another. Make, model, year and variant can affect fitment and wiring.

Is a detachable towbar better?
