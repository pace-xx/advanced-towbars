Towbar Installation Cost in Melbourne

Towbar installation is not a one-price-fits-all job. The correct quote depends on the vehicle, towbar type, wiring requirements and any towing accessories you need.

What affects the cost?

The main variables are your vehicle make, model and model year; the compatible towbar design; the electrical wiring required; and whether your setup also needs equipment such as an electric brake controller. Rather than publishing a generic price that may not apply to your vehicle, we quote the complete job around your actual setup.

What to send us for an accurate quote

Tell us your vehicle make, model, model year and what you plan to tow or carry. If you already know you need a fixed or detachable solution, brake controller or another accessory, include that too.
