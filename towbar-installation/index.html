We Come To You

No travelling across town or leaving your vehicle at a workshop unnecessarily. Advanced Towbars offers free on-site fitting across Melbourne, with experienced installers fitting towbars and compatible towing equipment at your home or workplace.

Choosing the right towbar

The right setup depends on your vehicle, what you plan to tow and any additional equipment required. The team can assess your make and model, discuss your towing needs and recommend a suitable solution before installation.

How long does towbar installation take?

Most vehicles take roughly two to three hours. More complex installations may require additional time depending on the vehicle design and equipment being fitted.

What else can be fitted?

Advanced Towbars also supplies towing equipment and vehicle accessories including tow hitches, electric brake controllers, sway control kits, roof and bike racks, reverse cameras, nudge and bull bars and selected 4WD accessories.
