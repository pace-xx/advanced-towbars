What does a brake controller do?

A brake controller works with a compatible trailer braking system so braking effort can be applied to the trailer when the tow vehicle brakes. The right setup depends on the trailer, vehicle and braking system.

Do I need one?

Brake requirements depend on the trailer and its mass. The existing Advanced Towbars site discusses braking requirements for trailers above certain weight thresholds; because legal and vehicle requirements matter, the new site avoids reducing this to a one-line rule and recommends checking the applicable Victorian/Australian requirements for the exact trailer.

Professional installation

Controller location, wiring and compatibility matter. Advanced Towbars can assess the vehicle and intended towing use and fit the required equipment as part of the towing setup.

Before you book

Can you install a brake controller with my towbar?

Yes. Tell us about the vehicle and trailer when requesting your towbar quote.

Are brake controllers only for caravans?

No. They can be relevant to trailers using compatible electric braking systems, depending on the trailer and legal requirements.

What information should I provide?

Vehicle make, model and year, plus details of the trailer or caravan you intend to tow.
