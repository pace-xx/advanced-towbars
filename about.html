About Advanced Towbars

A Melbourne Family Business Built Around Cars

More than four decades of combined motor-industry experience, personalised advice and the convenience of professional on-site fitting.

More than towbar supply

Advanced Towbars is made up of people with more than four decades of combined experience in the motor industry. The business supplies towbars and vehicle accessories, but the service also includes advice, product selection and professional fitting.

Personalised advice

Customers can discuss their vehicle and intended use with the team before choosing a towbar or accessory. The aim is to match the equipment to the vehicle and the job rather than treating every installation as the same.

Professional on-site fitting

Advanced Towbars offers free on-site fitting, allowing installations to be completed at a customer’s home or workplace across the service area.

Ongoing support

The business also provides ongoing advice on towing and the accessories it supplies and fits.
